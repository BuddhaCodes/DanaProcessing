    An IDE that keeps playing.

    The sketch and its music keep going while you type. One switch runs and stops them, and tells you how long they've been live. F11 puts the visuals on the projector with your code floating on top.

    • Ctrl+Enter Hot reload, state kept
    • Shift+F5 Stop the sketch and its sound
    • F3 Why is it slow? fps, Draw() time, memory
